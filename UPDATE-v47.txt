Upload the contents of this ZIP into the folder that currently contains your website index.html. Replace index.html, styles.css, and sw.js. Add excel.js, excel-worker.js, and the entire vendor folder. Keep script.js, manifest.webmanifest, and all other existing files.

Excel: Export the complete checklist as .xlsx. Download the template or import .xlsx, .xls, or .csv files. Preview a worksheet, then add items or replace the checklist after confirmation. Category, Item, Quantity (0–99), and Packed (Yes/No or True/False) columns are supported; only Item is required. Missing quantities start at 0 and items are unchecked unless marked packed in the sheet. Imports are limited to 2 MB, 1,000 items, 10 worksheets, and 30 columns. Formula/error cells are rejected; use values. Files are read on the visitor's device. This is file import/export, not live synchronization with Microsoft Excel.

The sidebar toggle uses a centered SVG arrow, reverses when pinned, and has more room below the brand.

After deploying, accept the site's update prompt or refresh. Test on your hosted website rather than opening index.html as a local file. If the old version persists, close existing tabs and reopen or try incognito.
