Your information, explained

Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Camping Classics provides camping planning tools for people of all ages. This notice explains what stays on your device, what is sent to other services, and the choices you have. Contact the Camping Classics operator at campingclassics2026@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

The short version
  • Your planner, packing lists, saved trips and spreadsheet imports work on your device.
  • Optional analytics is off until a visitor chooses Allow cookies.
  • Feedback and online campground searches use outside services.
  • Anyone with a shared-trip link can read the trip details in that link.

1. Planning and files on your device

Trip names, dates, camper counts, checklists, packing progress, daily notes and interface preferences are stored in your browser. Camping Classics does not upload this planning data to an account or a trip database. Excel and CSV files are read in your browser; their contents are not uploaded to Camping Classics or Formspree. Exported spreadsheets and backups are saved to the destination you choose.

Saved information remains until you delete it, clear this site's browser data, or your browser removes it. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see it. Delete individual saved trips in Saved Trips. To remove all local information, clear Camping Classics site data in your browser settings; export any trips you want to keep first.

2. Shared-trip links

A shared link contains a readable, encoded copy of your trip details, checklist and daily notes. It is not encrypted or protected by a password. Share only information you want the recipient to see. Avoid names, contact details, home addresses and other private information. Deleting a trip on your device does not recall links or copies already shared. The part of the link after the # is processed in the recipient's browser, rather than sent as part of the initial website request.

3. Cookies, analytics and privacy signals

The cookie banner offers Allow cookies and Deny cookies. Denying optional cookies does not stop the planner. A necessary preference cookie, __Host-cc-analytics, remembers that choice for up to six months.

Google Analytics is loaded only when a visitor allows analytics cookies and their browser privacy signals permit tracking. It helps us understand page views and feature use. Google receives usage and connection information, such as browser/device details, cookie identifiers and approximate region. We do not intentionally include trip names, notes, spreadsheet contents or search coordinates in our custom analytics events. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in this website's configuration.

Choose Cookie settings in the footer to withdraw permission. Deny cookies disables future analytics from this site and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies in this browser. It does not automatically remove records already held by Google. We also keep optional analytics off when the browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. We do not sell personal information or use this site to serve targeted advertisements.

Analytics cookies are configured to expire after six months, although use may renew them. Analytics records are governed by the Google Analytics account's retention settings and Google's processing terms, not by the cookie expiry. Contact us about existing analytics data. See Google's privacy policy.

4. Feedback and direct contact

Visitors can send their usefulness rating, improvement area and optional short comment through Formspree. No name or email field is requested. Please do not include personal or sensitive information. Formspree receives the submitted answers and connection metadata, such as your IP address, for delivery and service security. The Camping Classics operator can read the submissions in Formspree and any enabled email notifications. We use them to improve the site.

Sending feedback is optional. The form asks for agreement before sending. You can withdraw permission or request deletion by contacting us with the approximate date and comment, so we can locate the submission. Do not send an identity document. If you email us directly, your address and message are handled by our email service to respond to you.

Feedback remains in the Formspree inbox, and any delivered email copies, until deleted. This website does not run an automatic deletion schedule. We review privacy requests and remove identifiable feedback when it is no longer needed for its purpose, subject to any applicable recordkeeping duties. Service-provider backup retention may differ. See Formspree's privacy policy.

5. Campground searches

A city or ZIP search sends your query to Zippopotam.us or OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. Nearby-campground searches send location coordinates or a search area to Overpass API and, if necessary, Nominatim. These providers also receive normal connection metadata. Opening the map sends tile requests for the visible area to OpenStreetMap. Use My Location asks your browser for permission and rounds your coordinates to the nearest tenth of a degree on your device before searching; you can decline and use a city search instead.

Camping Classics keeps the current search location in page memory only, including for retrying a search, and does not save it as a trip or in browser storage. Reloading the page clears that search state. Data services may keep their own operational logs. Opening Google Maps or a campground website takes you to that provider, whose privacy rules apply. See OpenStreetMap's privacy policy.

6. Hosting, external links and essential requests

Cloudflare hosts and delivers this website. Like other web hosts, it processes connection and security data, including IP addresses, request times and browser headers, to deliver pages and protect its service. These essential requests happen when any visitor loads the site. They are separate from optional Google Analytics. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The slideshow photos and application files are served from Camping Classics. Viewing them does not load a social-media widget or contact the photographers' websites. Photographer credits, Instagram, reference links and maps are ordinary external links; opening them contacts the linked service.

7. Children and younger visitors

People of all ages can read the guides and use the planner, checklist and games information. Children should plan outdoor activities with a responsible adult.

We do not ask for your age when you choose cookie settings or send feedback. Please do not submit a child's name, exact location or other personal information in feedback.

If you believe a child has sent personal information, contact campingclassics2026@gmail.com with enough detail to locate it. A parent or guardian can request review, correction, deletion and an end to further collection of the child's information. We may need proportionate information to confirm the relationship before disclosing any records. We will investigate and remove improperly collected information rather than use it for marketing.

8. Your rights and choices

You may use the local planner without optional analytics, decline feedback, deny location access, delete local saved trips, and contact us about information you have sent. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your privacy regulator. We will handle requests under applicable law and explain any limits, including where we cannot identify an anonymous submission. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Where European or UK data-protection law applies, we rely on consent for optional analytics and voluntary feedback, and on applicable lawful grounds for delivering requested services and securing the site. We use the information only for the purposes described here. Contact us to exercise a right or discuss the basis for a particular use.

9. International processing and retention

Our hosting, feedback and analytics providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Their privacy notices describe their processing locations and safeguards. We do not claim that accepting cookies waives your privacy rights. Local trips stay until removed as described above; cookie preferences last up to six months; feedback and provider logs follow the retention arrangements described in this notice. Contact us for questions about transfers, providers or deletion.

10. Changes and contact

We will update this page when data practices change and show the new effective date. Material changes to optional uses will require a fresh choice where required. Privacy requests and parental inquiries: campingclassics2026@gmail.com.
