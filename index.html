    Beginner Guides

    Camping basics in simple language

    ⛺

    Setting Up Camp

    Choose a flat area, keep your sleeping area dry, and organize your gear before dark.

    🌧️

    Preparing for Rain

    Pack a rain jacket, keep clothes in sealed bags, and avoid placing your tent in low areas.

    🧭

    Campground Basics

    Know your campsite rules, keep your site clean, and respect quiet hours and nearby campers.

    🗑️

    Leave No Trace

    Pack out your trash, avoid damaging plants, and leave the campsite as clean as you found it.

    🔦

    Nighttime Setup

    Keep a flashlight close, organize shoes and gear, and make sure everyone knows the campsite layout.

    🥾

    What Beginners Forget

    Extra socks, a backup light, water, trash bags, weather-ready clothing, and a simple first-aid kit.

    Camping Games

    Easy activities for groups

    Start with six favorites, then open the rest if you want more ideas.

    Imposter

    Everyone gets the same secret word except one person. Players give short clues, then try to figure out who the imposter is before the imposter guesses the word.

    Best for 4+ players

    Among Us — Camp Edition

    Assign simple crewmate tasks around camp while one or two imposters secretly try to blend in. Hold quick discussion rounds and vote on who seems suspicious.

    Best for larger groups

    Charades

    Act out a word, movie, animal, activity, or camping-themed prompt without speaking while your team tries to guess it.

    No equipment needed

    Camp Olympics

    Create a few harmless mini-events such as a sock toss, balance challenge, trivia round, or fastest sleeping-bag roll-up.

    Good for teams

    Two Truths and a Lie

    Each person says three statements about themselves. Two are true and one is made up. Everyone guesses the lie.

    Great icebreaker

    Story Chain

    One person starts a story with one sentence. Each player adds one sentence, creating a completely unpredictable group story.

    Best around the campfire

    Would You Rather?

    Take turns asking two funny or difficult choices. Everyone picks one and explains why.

    Good for any group size

    20 Questions

    One person thinks of an object, place, or character. Everyone else gets up to twenty yes-or-no questions to figure it out.

    Easy around camp

    Campground Scavenger Hunt

    Create a list of safe things to spot, such as a pinecone, bird, trail sign, interesting rock, or a certain color in nature.

    Great during daylight

    Flashlight Tag

    In an open, permitted area after dark, one player searches while others stay within agreed boundaries. Keep the area hazard-free and follow campground quiet-hour rules.

    Outdoor nighttime game

    Categories

    Choose a category such as foods, animals, movies, or places. Go around the group naming something new until someone repeats or gets stuck.

    Fast and simple

    Guess the Sound

    One player makes a simple sound using nearby safe objects. Everyone else tries to identify what made it.

    Funny with groups
