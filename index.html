    Beginner Guides

    Camping basics in simple language

    A little know-how for a more comfortable first trip.

    Swipe left or right to exploreUse the arrows to explore

    ⛺01

    Setting Up Camp

    Choose a flat spot, settle in before dark, and give everything a place.

    Read more

    Try these simple steps

    1. Use the designated tent area and look for a flat spot. Avoid low spots where rainwater collects.
    2. Put up your tent before dark so you have time to check the setup instructions.
    3. Arrange your sleeping gear, then give shoes, lights, and bags a place you can find easily.
    🌧️02

    Preparing for Rain

    Keep your sleeping space and spare clothes dry when the weather changes.

    Read more

    Try these simple steps

    1. Check the forecast before leaving and bring a rain jacket.
    2. Keep spare clothes and sleeping gear in waterproof or sealed bags.
    3. Set up your rainfly according to your tent instructions. Keep gear away from the tent walls and avoid low ground.
    🧭03

    Campground Basics

    Know the rules, find the facilities, and be a considerate neighbor.

    Read more

    Try these simple steps

    1. Read the campground rules when you arrive, including quiet hours and any current fire restrictions.
    2. Find the toilets, drinking-water point, and trash area. Let your group know where they are.
    3. Keep your campsite tidy and give nearby campers space. Follow the campground’s food-storage instructions.
    🗑️04

    Leave No Trace

    Enjoy your campsite and leave it ready for the next campers.

    Read more

    Try these simple steps

    1. Bring trash bags and collect food scraps, wrappers, and other rubbish before you leave.
    2. Stay on established paths and use designated camping areas to protect plants.
    3. Leave rocks, plants, and natural objects where you find them. Give wildlife space and do not feed it.
    🔦05

    Nighttime Setup

    Put the things you will need after dark within easy reach.

    Read more

    Try these simple steps

    1. Keep a flashlight or headlamp close to your sleeping spot and check that it works.
    2. Put shoes and a warm layer where you can find them without emptying your bag.
    3. Before dark, show everyone the path between your tent and the campground facilities. Keep paths clear of loose gear.
    🥾06

    What Beginners Forget

    Do one last check for the small things that make a big difference.

    Read more

    Try these simple steps

    1. Check your list for spare socks, weather-ready layers, and a backup light.
    2. Bring drinking water, trash bags, and a simple first-aid kit.
    3. Walk through a pretend day at camp: sleeping, eating, getting dressed, and cleaning up. Add anything you would reach for to your checklist.

    Camping Games

    Easy activities for groups

    Find your next camp favorite. Pick a game and open the instructions when you’re ready.

    Swipe left or right to exploreUse the arrows to explore

    🌙01

    Mafia

    Secret roles, clever questions, and a group vote. Can the town find the mafia?

    Players
    6+ people
    Bring
    Paper slips + a pen
    Read more

    How to play

    1. For a simple six-person round, choose one moderator. Secretly assign one mafia role and four town roles using paper slips.
    2. At “night,” everyone closes their eyes. The moderator asks the mafia to look up and silently choose someone to leave the round, then close their eyes.
    3. At “day,” the moderator announces who is out. The remaining players discuss and vote someone out; reveal that person’s role. On a tied vote, nobody leaves.
    4. Repeat night and day. The town wins by voting out the mafia. The mafia wins when only one town player remains. People who are out watch quietly until the next round.
    5. For a larger group, agree on the roles first; two mafia work well with eight or more active players. The mafia wins when its number matches the remaining town players.
    🃏02

    UNO

    Match colors and numbers, play action cards, and race to empty your hand.

    Players
    2–10 players
    Bring
    An UNO deck
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Deal seven cards to each player. Make a face-down draw pile and turn over its top card to start a discard pile.
    2. Take turns matching the top discard by color, number, or symbol, or play an allowed Wild card. Follow your deck’s rules for action cards.
    3. If you have no playable card, draw one. You may play it immediately if it fits; otherwise your turn ends.
    4. Say “UNO” when you have one card left. The first person with an empty hand wins the round. For scoring and special-card details, use the rules that came with your deck.

    Official UNO rules (PDF)

    💬03

    Telephone

    Pass a whispered message around the group and see how much it changes.

    Players
    4+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Sit in a line or circle. The first person thinks of a short, funny sentence.
    2. Whisper it once to the next person. Each person quietly passes along what they heard.
    3. The last person says the message aloud. Compare it with the original, then choose a new starting player.
    🕵️04

    Imposter

    Give clues about a secret word and spot the player who does not know it.

    Players
    4+ players
    Bring
    Paper slips + a pen
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Prepare one slip per player. Put the same secret word on every slip except one, which says “imposter.” Shuffle and hand them out.
    2. Go around the circle giving one short clue each without saying the word. The imposter tries to blend in.
    3. Discuss the clues and vote. If the group finds the imposter, give that player one chance to guess the secret word before revealing it.
    🚀05

    Among Us — Camp Edition

    Finish small tasks around camp while a secret imposter tries to blend in.

    Players
    6+ players
    Bring
    Role slips + task list
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Choose a host and an agreed play area. Give everyone a secret role, with one imposter and the rest crewmates.
    2. Set simple tasks such as sorting cards or counting tent pegs. The imposter pretends to complete tasks; keep this version contact-free.
    3. After a few minutes, stop for a discussion and vote. Reveal the voted player’s role, then start another round with new roles.
    🎭06

    Charades

    Act out a prompt without speaking while your team tries to guess.

    Players
    4+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Split into two teams. The other team gives the actor a word, animal, movie, or camping activity.
    2. Act it out without words or sound effects. Give the team one minute to guess.
    3. Award a point for a correct answer, then switch teams and actors. Play to an agreed score.
    🏅07

    Camp Olympics

    Turn a few simple challenges into a friendly team competition.

    Players
    4+ players
    Bring
    Socks + camp gear
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Choose three easy events, such as a rolled-sock toss, trivia round, and sleeping-bag roll-up.
    2. Split into teams and agree on a score for each event. Use a clear space away from other campers.
    3. Let everyone try each event, add the points, and celebrate the team with the highest total.
    🤔08

    Two Truths and a Lie

    Share three things about yourself and let the group find the made-up one.

    Players
    3+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Think of two true statements about yourself and one believable made-up statement.
    2. Say all three in any order. Let the group ask a few questions and guess the lie.
    3. Reveal the answer, share the story if you like, and move to the next person.
    📖09

    Story Chain

    Build a silly group story, one unexpected sentence at a time.

    Players
    3+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. One player starts with a single sentence, such as “We opened the tent and found a tiny dragon.”
    2. Go around the group. Each player adds one sentence that connects to the story.
    3. After a few rounds, let the next player give the story an ending. Start again with a new theme.
    ↔️10

    Would You Rather?

    Pick between two funny choices and explain your answer.

    Players
    2+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Offer two choices, such as camping by a lake or camping in the mountains.
    2. Let everyone choose and explain why. Keep questions comfortable for the group and allow anyone to pass.
    3. Take turns asking the next question. There are no points or wrong answers.
    ❓11

    20 Questions

    Use yes-or-no questions to uncover a mystery object, place, or character.

    Players
    2+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. One person secretly chooses an object, place, or character.
    2. The group can ask up to twenty questions that can be answered with yes or no. Count each guess as a question.
    3. If someone guesses correctly, they choose the next mystery. Otherwise, reveal the answer after question twenty.
    🍃12

    Campground Scavenger Hunt

    Look for little details in nature and compare what your group finds.

    Players
    2+ players
    Bring
    A short list + a pen
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Make a short list of things to spot, such as a pinecone, bird, trail sign, or something yellow.
    2. Agree on boundaries and a return time. Stay on permitted paths and play during daylight.
    3. Tick off what you see or take photos. Leave natural objects where they are, then compare your finds.
    🔦13

    Flashlight Tag

    Try a slow, quiet spotting game within an agreed area after dark.

    Players
    4+ players
    Bring
    Flashlights
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Use a permitted, open area with clear boundaries, away from roads, water, fire, and trip hazards. Skip this game if there is no suitable space.
    2. Choose one seeker. Others walk to a spot within the boundaries; keep the game walking-only and respect quiet hours.
    3. The seeker spots someone with the light and says their name. Keep light out of people’s eyes. Switch seekers after the round.
    💡14

    Categories

    Keep the circle going by naming something new in the chosen category.

    Players
    3+ players
    Bring
    No equipment
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Choose a category such as animals, foods, movies, or things you bring camping.
    2. Go around the circle naming one new example each. Give each player a few seconds to think.
    3. If someone repeats an answer or gets stuck, choose a new category and let that player start.
    👂15

    Guess the Sound

    Listen closely and guess what made a mystery sound.

    Players
    3+ players
    Bring
    Safe everyday objects
    Read more

    How to play

    1. Gather a few harmless objects, such as a zipper, water bottle, or crinkly wrapper.
    2. One person makes a sound out of sight. Everyone else takes a guess.
    3. Reveal the object and switch sound-makers. Keep the sounds quiet enough for nearby campers.
