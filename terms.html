Planning tools for every age

The planner, packing checklist, guides and games information are available to all ages. Children should use camping advice and plan activities with a responsible adult. Optional feedback, online campground searches and analytics are available only after the privacy check for visitors aged 16 or older. The check is not parental consent. Check conditions before you go

Packing suggestions and guides are general information, not a complete safety assessment, professional instruction or emergency service. Check current weather, fire restrictions, permits, campsite rules, equipment instructions and your group's needs with the relevant authorities. Campground listings come from outside sources and may be incomplete or outdated. Confirm availability, access and facilities with the campground.

Your trips and shared links

You keep control of the trip content you enter. It is stored in your browser and may be lost if you clear site data, change devices or your browser removes it. Export backups for trips you want to keep. Share only content you have permission to share, and avoid private information. Anyone with a shared-trip link can read and copy its contents; deleting your local trip does not delete recipients' copies.

Respectful use

Do not use the site to send unlawful, harmful or unwanted material, impersonate others, probe other people's data or disrupt the service. Please keep feedback relevant and free of personal or sensitive information. Third-party services have their own terms and privacy practices.

We aim to keep the tools useful, but cannot promise uninterrupted service, complete campground data or error-free suggestions. Features may change. Nothing in these terms removes rights or remedies that applicable law does not allow us to exclude. These terms do not replace your own judgment or the supervision needed for a safe camping trip.
